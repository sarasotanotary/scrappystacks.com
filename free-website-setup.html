Before You Ask

A few honest answers.

I've never built a website. Will I actually be able to do this?

Yes. The guide is written for someone who has never touched code, in plain language, one step at a time. If you can follow a recipe, you can follow this.

What's the catch with "free hosting, forever"?

No catch. The guide uses GitHub Pages, a hosting service that's genuinely free with no time limit and no upgrade push. You only ever pay if you choose to register a domain name, which is optional and separate.

How long does this actually take?

Most people get a working site live in an afternoon. It's broken into short chapters, so it's easy to stop and pick back up.

Do I need to already own a domain?

No. The guide covers both: connecting a domain you already have, and what to do if you haven't registered one yet.

What if I'd rather just have it done for me?

That's an option too — see Build It For Me. But this guide is free, and most people are surprised how far they get on their own.
